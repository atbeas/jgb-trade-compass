Where to start, in North San Diego County

Specific to JGB already being at MiraCosta in Oceanside — several of these are literally a call away. These split into three real patterns: the union trades (electrician, plumber, elevator) put you straight into a paid apprenticeship where classroom hours are already built in — apply directly, no separate CC step needed. Others (HVAC, welding, diesel, aviation) run through MiraCosta/Palomar/Miramar coursework first, because that's what makes you hireable or sponsorable at all. And IT-type paths work best running school and a real job side by side.

On plumbing and elevator specifically: MiraCosta doesn't have a real hands-on plumbing program — what shows up under its name is a generic self-paced online "Foundations of Plumbing" course (ed2go), a beginner intro kit, not trade training. And no school in the region — MiraCosta included — teaches elevator work directly, and IUEC Local 18 is the only union apprenticeship. But it's not the only legal way in: California's Cal/OSHA elevator certification (CCCM) has a second track that qualifies someone through 3 years of documented on-the-job experience at any employer, no registered apprenticeship required — so a non-union San Diego company (24 Hour Elevator, Republic Elevator, San Diego Elevator & Lifts, Atlas Elevator, Advantage Elevator, among others) can legally hire and train someone toward full certification without ever going through Local 18. The tradeoff: no guaranteed union wage steps, training quality depends entirely on that one employer, and the state still has to approve the "proof of competency" — but it removes the biggest bottleneck in the union route, which is simply waiting for Local 18 to open a recruitment window.

Weigh the degree-heavy paths, too

For comparison — health, nutrition, fitness, and the math/science strength

Trades aren't the only thing on the table. JGB being genuinely into health, nutrition, and fitness — and genuinely good at math and science — points at a real cluster of longer, degree-heavy careers worth comparing honestly against the trade paths above, cost and time included.

The local bridge here is almost as clean as MiraCosta's own trade programs: MiraCosta's Kinesiology courses are explicitly built toward personal training, nutrition/dietetics, athletic training, and physical therapy, with a guaranteed transfer (AA-T) into Cal State San Marcos's Kinesiology B.S. — ten minutes from MiraCosta, and itself explicitly built as pre-PT prep.

If the math/science side pulls harder than the patient-care side, UC San Diego's Bioengineering program (ranked top-5 nationally) is the direct bridge between "good at math and science" and the health passion — building the technology instead of using it on patients.
